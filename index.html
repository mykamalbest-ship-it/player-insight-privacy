Player Insight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Player Insight is an internal browser extension intended for authorized employees. It operates on the private 777 and Vegas administrative panels and helps employees analyze information that is already available to them through those systems.

Data processed

To provide its functionality, Player Insight may process information displayed in the supported administrative panels, including player identifiers, gaming activity, bets, deposits and withdrawals, bonus information, and VIP-manager comments or related internal information.

Purpose of processing

This information is processed only to provide Player Insight's core functionality, including displaying summaries and analytics inside the administrative interface.

Access and authorization

Player Insight does not provide access to the 777 or Vegas administrative systems. Users must already be authorized to access the applicable administrative panel.

Data storage and sharing

Player Insight does not sell user data. The extension is designed to process supported administrative-panel data for its functionality and does not use that data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or unrelated purposes.

Website access

The extension is designed to operate only on the supported private administrative panels at admin.777.ua and admin.vegas.ua.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Player Insight functionality or its data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding Player Insight, contact the organization or administrator that provided you with access to the extension.

This page describes the intended data practices of Player Insight. The publisher should ensure that it remains accurate for every released version of the extension.
